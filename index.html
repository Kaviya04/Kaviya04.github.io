Research

03 / 05
Ongoing · Independent Research · KIT

Who Carries the Risk? Interdependencies in AI Data-Centre Debt Financing

AI data centres are increasingly financed through structured debt — special purpose vehicles, long-term hyperscaler leases, parent guarantees and other credit enhancements. These agreements tie developers, tenants, guarantors and lenders closely together. My research maps those interdependencies: how the parties are contractually linked, and how a change in one party's financial position — a tenant's credit quality, a guarantor's balance sheet, a developer's leverage — transmits through the structure to the others.

The aim is to understand where risk actually sits in these deals, and whether ratings and market pricing reflect that chain of dependence. Supervised by Prof. Dr. Martin Ruckes, Karlsruhe Institute of Technology.
